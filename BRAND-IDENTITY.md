# Karimi Auto — Brand Identity & Positioning

## Brand Name

**Arabic:** كريمي أوطو
**Latin:** Karimi Auto
**Tagline (AR):** خبراء حماية الطوموبيل فالمغرب
**Tagline (FR):** Expert en protection automobile au Maroc

---

## Positioning

**One-liner:** كريمي أوطو هو المرجع ديال حماية الطوموبيل فالمغرب — منتجات مختبرة ومعتمدة للمناخ المغربي، بخبرة متخصصة وضمان حقيقي.

**Category:** متخصصون فحماية السيارات (Car Protection Specialists)

**Not a random online store.** Karimi Auto is positioned as the Moroccan authority on car protection — like a trusted offline accessory shop your neighbor recommends, but online with COD convenience.

**Key differentiators:**
1. **Specialist, not generalist** — Only car protection products (heat, paint, theft). Not "everything for cars."
2. **Climate-tested** — Every product is validated for Moroccan conditions: extreme heat, sand, dust, UV.
3. **Expert voice** — We explain *why* things work (science, materials), not just hype.
4. **Local trust signals** — Moroccan phone confirmation, COD, 30-day warranty, Darija communication.

---

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- **Who:** Moroccan men, 25–45 years old
- **Where:** Urban — Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Agadir, Fes
- **Car:** Mid-range (Dacia, Renault, Hyundai, Toyota, VW) — their car is their investment
- **Mindset:** "My car reflects who I am. I want to protect it but I don't trust random online stores."
- **Pain:** Burned steering wheels, scratched paint, fear of theft — real daily frustrations
- **What they need to see:** Expertise, social proof from other Moroccans, no-risk purchase (COD + guarantee)

---

## Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Expert advisor, not salesman. Confident but warm. Like a trusted mechanic friend.
- **Language:** Moroccan Darija Arabic — natural, not MSA-formal. Uses French-loan words Moroccans actually use (طوموبيل, كليماتيزور, كاروسري, ليفرور).
- **Never:** Desperate, over-discounting, generic, corporate-stiff.
- **Always:** Specific (numbers, data, stats), empathetic (we know the problem), authoritative (we tested it).

## Visual Identity

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Primary** | `#0F2B46` | Deep navy — authority, trust, automotive professionalism |
| **Primary Dark** | `#081A2E` | Hover states, deeper emphasis |
| **Accent** | `#C8963E` | Warm gold — premium, highlight, CTAs, stars |
| **Background** | `#F5F3F0` | Warm off-white — clean, professional, not sterile |
| **Surface** | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, modals |
| **Ink** | `#161616` | Primary text |
| **Muted** | `#5E625F` | Secondary text |
| **Success** | `#168A4A` | Trust, guarantees, verified |
| **Urgency** | `#B42318` | Stock warnings, scarcity |
| **Border** | `#E2DDD6` | Card borders, dividers |

**Why navy + gold?**
- Navy (`#0F2B46`) signals trust, stability, and expertise — used by banks, premium auto brands, and established Moroccan businesses.
- Gold (`#C8963E`) adds warmth and a premium feel — signals quality without looking cheap.
- The combination feels like a professional automotive specialist, not a generic dropship store.

### Typography

- **Arabic:** Cairo (headings) + Tajawal (body) — clean, modern, excellent Arabic rendering
- **Latin:** Inter — professional, pairs well with Arabic fonts
- **Direction:** RTL (Right-to-Left)

### Logo

- Circle badge with **K** in gold on navy background
- Arabic brand name: كريمي أوطو
- Subtitle: خبراء حماية الطوموبيل

## Brand Pillars (used across all copy)

1. **خبرة متخصصة** (Specialist Expertise) — We only do car protection. We know it deeply.
2. **مختبر للمغرب** (Tested for Morocco) — Our products survive 70°C, sand storms, and UV.
3. **بلا مخاطرة** (Zero Risk) — COD + 30-day warranty + phone confirmation.
4. **+3,200 سائق مغربي** (Social Proof) — Real customers, real cities, verified results.
